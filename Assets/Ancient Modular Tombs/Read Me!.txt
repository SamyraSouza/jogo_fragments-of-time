# Asset Setup Guide: Post-Processing & URP Configuration

Thank you for purchasing this asset! This package comes with pre-configured, high-fidelity visual profiles to deliver a clean cinematic look out of the box. 

All configuration assets are located in the `/Profiles` directory. Follow this quick guide to apply them to your scene.

---

## 1. Apply the Pre-Configured URP Asset

To ensure all custom rendering settings, textures, and capabilities match the intended design, you need to activate the included URP configuration.

1. Go to **Edit -> Project Settings -> Graphics**.
2. Locate the **Scriptable Render Pipeline Settings** field at the top.
3. Drag and drop the **Universal Render Pipeline Asset** from your `/Profiles` folder into this field.

---

## 2. Setup Post-Processing & Camera

Your scene camera needs to be configured to process the cinematic effects.

1. Select your **Main Camera** in the Hierarchy.
2. In the Inspector, navigate to the **Camera** component.
3. Under the **Rendering** section, make sure the **Post Processing** checkbox is **checked**.
4. *(Highly Recommended)* Set **Anti-aliasing** to **Temporal Anti-aliasing (TAA)** to eliminate pixel flickering on fine interior details and geometry.

---

## 3. Activate the Global Volume Profile

We have already tuned the Tonemapping (ACES), Bloom, and Color Adjustments for you inside the `PostFX Profile`.

1. Right-click in your Hierarchy window and select **Volume -> Global Volume**.
2. Select the newly created `Global Volume` GameObject.
3. In the Inspector, ensure its **Mode** is set to **Global**.
4. Drag and drop the **PostFX Profile** asset from `/Profiles` directly into the **Profile** slot of the Volume component.

---

## Troubleshooting

* **The screen remains blank or effects don't apply?**
  Ensure the **Layer** of your `Global Volume` object (found at the very top of the Inspector) is set to **Default**. Your Main Camera's *Volume Mask* is set to look for the *Default* layer by default.
* **Are you experiencing grainy lightmaps?**
  If you decide to re-bake lighting for this interior scene, go to *Window -> Rendering -> Lighting* and ensure **Compress Lightmaps** is turned **Off** for pristine, artifact-free baking results.
